What I'm Up To Now

9.30.26: This year, I have the honor of running sound at most of the UMass SALT shows, and possibly yours as well! If you need PA services for your own show, email me with your prospective location/date and bill. I provide front-of-house PA, stage monitors, microphones and stands, and mixing/set change services the entire night. I also record stems for every band, which can be used for demos or promotions!

If you're a musician or band looking to record demos or material without an audience and four other bands sharing your space, feel free to reach out as well- all the same care and experience apply in these settings as well! I'm happy to help contribute to the local scene any way I can in thanks for all it has done for myself and my children. -Shalin

I've also been working on an upcoming record with Scotty- keep visiting this page for updates on that front! Classes have been intense but I'll be adding more to this page whenever I can.

Previous Announcements

8.2026: I had a lovely time playing bass with Corsica this summer- thank you to Connor, Mike, Logan, Singe, and Waffle House for making this all possible! Rest in peace 2011 Hyundai, you will be sorely missed. Many memories of sweat and doom were made within you.

4.30.2026: This week has been incredibly hectic. Last Saturday, I played with Corsica for the first time at the Hampshire Solar Canopy; yesterday, I ran and performed at my own band's EP release show; today, I'm headed to Boston with my friends Sharada and Lili to see Thursday (on a Thursday!); and tomorrow, I'm playing in Cold Spring Hollow with Corsica again. The last two months, all of my free time has gone into recording, mixing, and mastering Experiment One's first EP, Tragedy in the Herter Basement. DuBois Library. I'm so grateful for the few dozen people who donated to our fund and the ~70ish people who showed up to our show at Prescott! Listening links can be found if you scroll down a bit, and if you'd like a CD for purchase, you can find them at our Bandcamp.
