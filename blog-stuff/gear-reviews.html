Gear Reviews

Microphones, guitars, basses, pedals, amps. Primarily for my own reference, and as such reflect my own limited knowledge/abilities.

Sennheiser e906 Dynamic Mic

Bought used from Guitar Center in August 2026

Very convenient. I can't tell if it has a compressed sound or if that's just the way my main overdrive sounds. The switchable high end taper/boost switch is also very useful, as the middle setting is somewhat dark.


Fender Custom '68 Pro Reverb

Bought used from Reverb in August 2026

Much lighter and smaller than my Twin Reverb. Fairly loud when it starts breaking up, but I can use it at very low volumes with my pedalboard in my room. Only noticeable problem is the occasional tube rattle at certain volumes, and the weak tremolo ("vibrato") effect.


Way Huge Conspiracy Theory

Bought used from Guitar Center in July 2026

This is a relatively compact Klon clone. It's incredibly versatile for a lot of different purposes. Currently I have it at the start of my chain, barely on, for a tiny bit of crunch so the Fender amp isn't 100% clean. However, for a time I also ran it at the end of the chain, after my distortion. In a standard Fender amp, there's a bright cap that makes the amp sound fizzy with distortion; the larger ones, like the Twin, have this cap on a switch to toggle on/off. The Klon apparently has some sort of filter that negates this bright cap, as even with my Twin set to 'Bright,' running my EVH distortion into the Klon attenuated a lot of that harsh high end. This also makes it useful as a light/medium gain platform, with the Boss Blues Drive behind it to make it more intense. A very useful rhythm guitar pedal. Falls apart a little at max gain; becomes a little mushy.
